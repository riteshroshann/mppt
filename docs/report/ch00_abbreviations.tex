AC & Alternating Current. An electric current whose direction reverses periodically. \\
ADC & Analogue-to-Digital Converter. A circuit that converts a continuous voltage into a number a processor can read. \\
CCM & Continuous Conduction Mode. Operating condition of a switching converter in which the inductor current never falls to zero within a switching period. \\
CVD & Colour Vision Deficiency. Reduced ability to distinguish certain colours; considered when choosing figure colours. \\
DC & Direct Current. An electric current of constant direction. \\
DCM & Discontinuous Conduction Mode. The complementary case to CCM, in which inductor current reaches zero and stays there for part of the switching period. \\
DSP & Digital Signal Processor. A microprocessor specialised for real-time arithmetic, used here as the control processor. \\
EMI & Electromagnetic Interference. Unwanted radiated or conducted electrical noise. \\
ESC & Extremum Seeking Control. A feedback method that drives a system to the extremum of a measured performance function without knowing that function. \\
ESR & Equivalent Series Resistance. The effective resistance in series with an ideal capacitor or inductor. \\
GaN & Gallium Nitride. A wide-bandgap semiconductor used for fast, low-loss power transistors. \\
IGBT & Insulated Gate Bipolar Transistor. A power switching device suited to higher voltages. \\
INC & Incremental Conductance. An MPPT algorithm that compares the incremental conductance $\mathrm{d}I/\mathrm{d}V$ with the instantaneous conductance $-I/V$. \\
I--V & Current--Voltage. Refers to the current-versus-voltage characteristic curve of a solar device. \\
LC & Inductor--Capacitor. Refers to the resonant circuit formed by an inductor and a capacitor. \\
LHP & Left Half Plane. The region of the complex plane with negative real part; poles there correspond to stable modes. \\
LPV & Linear Parameter Varying. A model that is linear for each fixed value of a parameter, but whose coefficients depend on that parameter. \\
LTI & Linear Time Invariant. A system whose behaviour is linear and does not change with time. \\
MOSFET & Metal Oxide Semiconductor Field Effect Transistor. The transistor used as the controlled switch in the converter. \\
MPP & Maximum Power Point. The single operating point on a photovoltaic I--V curve at which output power is greatest. \\
MPPT & Maximum Power Point Tracking. The control function that keeps a photovoltaic source operating at or near its MPP. \\
NOCT & Nominal Operating Cell Temperature. The cell temperature reached under a defined reference environment, used to estimate cell temperature in the field. \\
ODE & Ordinary Differential Equation. \\
P\&O & Perturb and Observe. An MPPT algorithm that repeatedly perturbs the operating point and keeps moving in whichever direction increased power. \\
PCB & Printed Circuit Board. \\
PI & Proportional--Integral. A controller whose output is a weighted sum of the error and the integral of the error. \\
PID & Proportional--Integral--Derivative. A PI controller with an added derivative term; not used here. \\
PM & Phase Margin. The additional phase lag that would make a stable feedback loop marginally stable. \\
PS & Physical Signal. In Simscape, a directed signal carrying a physical quantity with units, as distinct from an electrical connection. \\
P--V & Power--Voltage. Refers to the power-versus-voltage characteristic curve of a solar device. \\
PV & Photovoltaic. Relating to the direct conversion of light into electricity. \\
PWM & Pulse Width Modulation. Encoding a continuous command as the duty ratio of a fixed-frequency rectangular wave. \\
RCC & Ripple Correlation Control. An MPPT method that correlates the converter's own switching ripple in power and voltage to estimate the power gradient. \\
RHP & Right Half Plane. The region of the complex plane with positive real part; a zero there fundamentally limits achievable bandwidth. \\
RK4 & Fourth-order Runge--Kutta. A numerical method for integrating ordinary differential equations. \\
RMS & Root Mean Square. \\
SiC & Silicon Carbide. A wide-bandgap semiconductor used for efficient power devices. \\
STC & Standard Test Conditions. \SI{1000}{\watt\per\square\metre} irradiance, \SI{25}{\celsius} cell temperature, air mass 1.5 spectrum. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
